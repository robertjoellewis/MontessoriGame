# Bluebonnet Montessori - Game Information

## Official Game Name
**Bluebonnet Montessori**

## Game Description
A cozy Montessori toddler classroom management simulation game where you play as Virginia, a Montessori guide teaching children in a warm, nurturing environment.

## Setting
- **Location**: Bluebonnet Montessori School
- **Time Period**: Contemporary
- **Theme**: Cozy, educational, nurturing

## Key Characters
- **Virginia**: The player character, a Montessori guide
- **Wade**: Lead Guide at Bluebonnet Montessori
- **12 Children**: Each with unique personalities, temperaments, and sensitive periods

## Audio
- **Classroom Music**: Curious & Gentle kalimba tones (100 BPM)
- **Naptime Music**: Peaceful & Dreamy lullaby (55 BPM)
- **Success Sound**: Magical sparkle chime
- **Failure Sound**: Gentle descending notes

## Locations
- Virginia's Cottage
- Village Street
- Bluebonnet Montessori School
  - Classroom
  - Nap Room
