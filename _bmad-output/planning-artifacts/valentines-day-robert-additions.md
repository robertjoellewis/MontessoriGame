## 💭 Design Philosophy

**Why Robert is in the prototype:**
1. **Personal Touch** - User (Robert) is in the game he's making for girlfriend
2. **Valentine's Gift** - Shows thoughtfulness and personal connection
3. **Future Promise** - Hints at relationship mechanics to come
4. **Cozy Vibes** - Not just work sim, also life sim with partner
5. **Realism** - Virginia actually lives with her boyfriend!

**Balance:**
- Not overwhelming (just visual presence)
- Not distracting from core gameplay (school mechanics)
- Romantic without being cheesy
- Sets up future mechanics naturally
