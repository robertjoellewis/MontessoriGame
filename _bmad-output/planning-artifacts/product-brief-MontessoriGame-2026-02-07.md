## Quick Context for Next Session

**What this is:** A cozy mobile-first HTML5 game where you play as a Montessori preschool guide. Think Stardew Valley meets classroom management, with gacha mechanics for collecting beautiful educational materials.

**Key Decisions Made:**
- Mobile-first HTML5, deploy on Heroku
- 14-minute days (Stardew style)
- 12 children max, toddler/preschool age (finalize: 18mo-3yr OR 3-5yr)
- Free game, gacha is earned through play (not monetized)
- Solo dev project
- Minimal home life in MVP (wake 7am, walk to school, arrive by 7:45am)
- Town life unlocks later

**Audiences:** Parents curious about Montessori, teachers, cozy game fans

**Core Loop:** Observe children → spot sensitive periods → present right lesson at right moment → watch them grow
