# Child Personality System — Toddler Classroom

**Date:** 2026-02-07
**Context:** 12 distinct children with personalities rooted in Montessori developmental theory

---

## Montessori Framework (The Science Behind It)

### Sensitive Periods (Toddler Age 18mo-3yr)
These are windows where children are naturally driven to learn specific things:

1. **Order** — Need for routine, predictability, things in their place
2. **Language** — Vocabulary explosion, naming everything
3. **Movement** — Walking, climbing, refining coordination
4. **Small Objects** — Fascination with tiny things, details
5. **Toilet Learning** — Independence in self-care
6. **Social Behavior** — Parallel play → cooperative play

**Game mechanic:** Each child has 2-3 active sensitive periods at any time. These shift slowly as they develop. Presenting lessons that match their sensitive period = massive success. Missing the window = wasted effort.

---

### Temperament Types (Based on Child Development Research)
1. **Cautious/Slow-to-Warm** — Needs time to trust, observes before doing
2. **Bold/Adventurous** — Jumps right in, takes risks
3. **Social/Gregarious** — Seeks out other children constantly
4. **Independent/Solitary** — Prefers working alone, deep concentration
5. **Sensitive** — Big emotions, easily overwhelmed
6. **Easy-Going** — Adaptable, goes with the flow

**Game mechanic:** Affects how children respond to lessons, transitions, and disruptions.

---

### Developmental Stage Within Toddler Range
Even within 18mo-3yr, there's huge variation:
- **Young toddler (18-24mo):** Just walking, limited language, short attention
- **Mid toddler (24-30mo):** Language explosion, growing independence, toilet learning
- **Older toddler (30-36mo):** Full sentences, cooperative play emerging, ready for complex work

**Game mechanic:** Determines which lessons they're ready for and how long they can concentrate.

## Gameplay Integration

### Observation Mechanic
**Hover over a child to see:**
- Current mood (happy, frustrated, tired, curious)
- Active sensitive period (highlighted if strong)
- What they're interested in right now ("watching Lily", "wants to pour", "needs a hug")

**Advanced observation unlocks:**
- Tier 2+: See their energy level (affects concentration duration)
- Tier 3+: See home context flags ("rough morning", "new sibling", "parent stressed")
- Tier 4: Predict upcoming sensitive period shifts

### Personality-Driven Events
**Examples:**
- Marcus climbs the shelf → choose how to respond (redirect gently vs. firm boundary vs. create safe climbing option)
- Sofia has a meltdown during transition → choose strategy (comfort, give space, adjust environment)
- Mia won't let Oliver use the puzzle → mediate conflict (talk to Mia, comfort Oliver, present a second puzzle)

**Choices matter:** Your response affects trust meter, child development, and relationship dynamics.

### Growth Tracking
Each child has progress bars in:
- Independence
- Concentration
- Social skills
- Confidence
- Specific curriculum areas (language, movement, etc.)

**Graduation moment:** After ~20 in-game days, Elijah (oldest) graduates to the primary classroom. Emotional cutscene. He's replaced by a new toddler (randomized personality).
