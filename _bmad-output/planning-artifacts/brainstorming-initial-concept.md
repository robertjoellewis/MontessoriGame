# 🌿 Montessori Preschool Cozy Game — Initial Brainstorm

**Date:** 2026-02-07
**Source:** Initial brainstorming session with Claude

---

## Core Concept

A cozy management/life sim where you play as a new lead guide at a small Montessori preschool. You learn the philosophy as you play — the game teaches you Montessori principles organically through gameplay, not lectures. You grow your classroom, collect and craft materials, observe children, and build a life in a charming small town.

### Design Pillars
- No prior Montessori knowledge required — the game is the teacher
- Collection / gacha mechanics for materials and furnishings
- Deep unlock trees tied to your growth as a guide
- Cozy vibes: no fail states, gentle progression
- Optional home life / town life layer

---

## 🎲 Core Gameplay Loop

### The Day Cycle
- **Morning Arrival** — Children arrive; you greet families, notice moods/energy
- **Work Cycle** — The long uninterrupted block. You observe, present lessons, set up materials. This is the strategic heart.
- **Circle Time / Outdoor Time** — Group activities, nature walks, songs
- **Afternoon** — Rest, pickup, parent conversations
- **After Hours** — Prep the environment, journal/reflect, go home

### What You Actually Do
- **Observe** — Watch children interact with materials. Spot "sensitive periods" (windows where a child is ready to learn something). This is the core Montessori skill and the game's central mechanic.
- **Present** — Give one-on-one or small group lessons. Timing matters: present the right material to the right child at the right moment → big payoff.
- **Prepare the Environment** — Place materials on shelves, arrange furniture, tend plants. The room is the curriculum.
- **Build Relationships** — With children, families, co-teachers, and the community.

## 🎰 Collection & Gacha Mechanics

### Material Collection
Montessori classrooms use very specific beautiful materials (Pink Tower, Golden Beads, Movable Alphabet, etc.). This is a natural collectible system.

**Sources for new materials:**
- Woodworker / Artisan in town — Commission custom materials (takes time + resources)
- Estate sales / antique shops — Rare vintage Montessori materials with bonus properties
- Catalogs — Order from suppliers (the "standard" acquisition path)
- DIY Crafting — Make your own from gathered resources (wood, paint, fabric, natural items)
- Mystery boxes from educational conferences — The gacha! Attend conferences → get randomized material packs with rarity tiers
- Seasonal / event materials — Holiday-themed, nature-themed rotating stock

### Rarity Tiers
- 🪵 **Handmade** — Basic, functional, gets the job done
- 🌿 **Classic** — Standard Montessori-quality materials
- ✨ **Heirloom** — Beautiful, high-quality, children are extra drawn to them
- 🌟 **Legacy** — Rare vintage or artisan pieces with unique visual flair and bonus effects

### What Rarity Does
Higher quality materials:
- Attract children's attention more easily
- Hold concentration longer
- Have prettier animations when children use them
- May unlock special "aha moment" cutscenes

## 🔓 Unlock Systems

### Guide Growth Tree
You start as a brand new assistant who doesn't know anything. Unlock tiers represent your journey:

1. **Observer** — You can watch but not present. Learning the basics.
2. **Assistant** — You can present simple Practical Life lessons.
3. **Guide-in-Training** — Sensorial and early math/language unlocked.
4. **Lead Guide** — Full curriculum access, can mentor new assistants.
5. **Master Guide** — Can design custom materials, host parent education nights, train other schools.

Each tier unlocks:
- New lesson types you can present
- New materials available to acquire
- New room configurations
- New interactions with children and families

### Curriculum Unlocks (5 Areas of Montessori)
Each is its own skill tree:
- 🧹 **Practical Life** — Pouring, polishing, food prep, care of self/environment
- 👁️ **Sensorial** — Pink Tower, Color Boxes, Sound Cylinders, etc.
- 🔢 **Math** — Golden Beads, Stamp Game, bead chains
- 📖 **Language** — Sandpaper Letters, Movable Alphabet, reading
- 🌍 **Cultural** — Geography, science, art, music

### Classroom Unlocks
- Start with a single small room
- Expand to indoor/outdoor flow
- Add a garden, a workshop, a nature area
- Unlock multi-age grouping (having 3-6 year olds together — a core Montessori thing)
- Unlock infant/toddler program, elementary program (endgame expansion?)

### Town / Reputation Unlocks
- As your school's reputation grows, new families move to town
- Each family brings a child with unique personality, interests, and challenges
- Unlocks new shops, community spaces, events

## 👶 The Children (Characters)

### Personality System
Each child has:
- **Temperament** — Cautious, bold, social, independent, etc.
- **Sensitive Periods** — Timed windows for order, language, movement, small objects, etc. You need to notice these (observation mechanic) and respond.
- **Interests** — Dinosaurs, cooking, bugs, music, building, etc. Affects which materials draw them.
- **Needs** — Comfort level, confidence, social connection, independence
- **Home Context** — Some kids are going through stuff (new sibling, parents divorcing, moving). Affects behavior.

### Growth / Graduation
- Children develop over in-game months/years
- You watch them go from needing help pouring water to reading books independently
- Graduation ceremonies are emotional milestone events
- Alumni occasionally visit or send letters (long-term payoff for investment)

## 🏠 Home Life (Optional Layer)

### Your Character
- Customize appearance
- Live in a small apartment/cottage in town
- Decorate and upgrade your home
- Have a morning routine (coffee, journaling, yoga?) that gives daily buffs
- Relationships with townspeople (not necessarily romance — friendships, mentorship)

### Town Activities
- Farmers market — buy supplies, ingredients for classroom cooking
- Nature trails — collect natural materials (pinecones, shells, leaves) for classroom
- Library — research new Montessori techniques (unlocks)
- Community events — fairs, seasonal celebrations
- Workshops — attend or host (professional development = unlock currency)

### Seasons & Weather
- Affect outdoor classroom activities
- Seasonal materials and nature collections
- Holiday/cultural celebration events in the classroom
- Weather affects children's moods and energy

## 💡 What Makes It Fun (Not Just Educational)

### Surprise & Delight Moments
- "Aha moment" cutscenes when a child has a breakthrough
- Unexpected child behaviors (funny, sweet, chaotic)
- Children teaching each other (emergent behavior you enabled)
- Parent thank-you notes, drawings from kids
- Discovering a child's hidden talent

### Light Strategy
- Limited shelf space — which materials do you put out this week?
- Balancing individual needs across 15-20 children
- Budget management (tuition, fundraising, material purchases)
- Staff hiring and training

### Social / Emotional Challenges
- A child who won't separate from their parent
- Two kids in conflict over a material
- A child who is "wandering" and can't settle
- A parent who wants worksheets and grades (gentle education required!)

These are puzzles, not punishments — figure out the Montessori approach and it works

### No Fail State, But...
- Children can regress or stagnate if neglected
- Parents can pull their kids if unhappy
- Your mentor gives gentle feedback
- The game nudges you toward good practice without punishing
