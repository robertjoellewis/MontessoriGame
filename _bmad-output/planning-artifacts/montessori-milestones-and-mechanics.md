## Nap Time Mechanic (12:15pm - 2:30pm)

### The Challenge

**Real-world context:** Getting all toddlers to fall asleep during nap time is one of the hardest parts of a Montessori guide's day. Usually 1-2 children are disruptive, cry, or struggle to settle down.

### Gameplay Implementation

**Time:** 12:15pm - 2:30pm (in-game day)

**Phase Breakdown:**
1. **Transition to Rest (12:15-12:30pm)** — Get children to their mats, calm the room
2. **Settling Period (12:30-1:00pm)** — Critical window where disruptions happen
3. **Deep Sleep (1:00-2:15pm)** — If successful, children are asleep and you have free time
4. **Wake Up (2:15-2:30pm)** — Gentle wake-up routine

### Challenge Mechanics

**Each child has a "settle difficulty" based on:**
- Temperament (Bold/Sensitive kids harder to settle)
- Energy level (high energy from morning activities = harder to sleep)
- Home context (bad morning at home = more likely to cry)
- Age (younger toddlers need more help)
- Mood (frustrated or overstimulated = won't settle)

**Disruption Types:**
- Crying/fussing
- Talking/singing when supposed to be quiet
- Getting up and wandering
- Waking other children
- Needing comfort (specific guide attention)

**Player Actions:**
- Rub a child's back (soothing, takes time)
- Whisper reassurance (quick but less effective)
- Move a disruptive child's mat (risk waking others)
- Adjust room (dim lights, soft music, temperature)
- Strategic pre-nap calm-down activities (reading, quiet songs)

### Success Tiers

**All children asleep within 30 minutes (by 1:00pm):**
- ⭐⭐⭐ **Perfect Nap**
- Reward: +3 Discovery Tokens, +10 Trust with lead guide, unlocks "Master of Rest" achievement
- Bonus: 1 hour 15 min of free time to prep materials, journal, or professional development

**10-11 children asleep, 1-2 need ongoing attention:**
- ⭐⭐ **Good Nap**
- Reward: +1 Discovery Token, +5 Trust
- You get 30-45 min of partial free time (must check on wakeful kids)

**8-9 children asleep, several struggling:**
- ⭐ **Challenging Nap**
- Reward: +5 Stars (regular currency), +2 Trust
- Minimal free time, you're managing disruptions the whole time

**Fewer than 8 children asleep:**
- ❌ **Nap Failure**
- Consequence: -2 Trust with lead guide (she has to step in), you learn from the experience
- Tooltip: "Reflect on what went wrong. Try a calmer morning routine next time."

### Strategy Depth

**Player learns to:**
- Give high-energy kids MORE active play in the morning (tire them out)
- Give sensitive kids LESS stimulation before nap (calm them down)
- Notice which children need comfort objects (blanket, stuffed animal)
- Identify "nap buddies" (children who settle better near each other)
- Use specific pre-nap routines (same story every day = predictability = easier sleep)

**Progression:**
- Tier 1 (Observer): You just watch, lead guide handles nap time
- Tier 2 (Assistant): You help with 3-4 children while lead guide manages the rest
- Tier 3 (Guide-in-Training): You manage nap time for half the class (6 children)
- Tier 4 (Lead Guide): You handle ALL 12 children solo

## Other Montessori-Specific Level Up Milestones

### 1. First Successful Three-Period Lesson
**What it is:** Classic Montessori teaching technique (Naming, Recognition, Recall)
- "This is red" (Naming)
- "Show me red" (Recognition)
- "What is this?" (Recall)

**Challenge:** Doing it smoothly without confusing the child

**Reward:**
- Unlock: Three-Period Lesson technique for all future lessons
- +2 Discovery Tokens
- Trust +5
- Achievement: "Montessori Fundamentals"

**When:** Tier 2 (Assistant) - This is your "graduation" from just observing

---

### 2. Child's First Independent Work Cycle
**What it is:** A child chooses a material, works with it deeply for 20+ minutes uninterrupted, then puts it away without being asked

**Challenge:** Creating the conditions where this happens (prepared environment, right material at right time, no interruptions)

**Reward:**
- +1 Discovery Token per child who achieves this milestone
- Trust +10
- Emotional cutscene showing the child's concentration and satisfaction
- Achievement: "Following the Child" (first time), "Master Observer" (all 12 children achieve it)

**When:** Tier 2-3, this is THE core Montessori moment

---

### 3. Successfully Redirect a Conflict Without Intervention
**What it is:** Two children want the same material. You set up the environment so they resolve it themselves (wait, take turns, find alternative)

**Challenge:** Resisting the urge to solve it FOR them, trusting the process

**Reward:**
- +1 Discovery Token
- Trust +5
- Both children gain +social skills, +patience
- Achievement: "Grace and Courtesy Guide"

**When:** Tier 3 (Guide-in-Training)

---

### 4. Parent Education Night (Successfully Explain Montessori)
**What it is:** A parent asks "Why don't you just teach them letters?" and you have to explain the Montessori philosophy clearly

**Challenge:** Dialogue choices - pick the right explanation without being condescending or confusing

**Reward:**
- +3 Discovery Tokens
- Trust +15 (huge boost)
- Unlock: "Parent Advocate" achievement
- New parents may enroll (classroom expansion opportunity)

**When:** Tier 3-4 (Lead Guide)

---

### 5. Design and Introduce a Custom Material
**What it is:** You create a new material tailored to a specific child's sensitive period (e.g., dinosaur-themed counting beads for a dino-obsessed kid)

**Challenge:** Requires resources, crafting time, AND observation to know what to make

**Reward:**
- Child's engagement and progress skyrockets
- +2 Discovery Tokens
- Trust +8
- Achievement: "Material Designer"
- Unlocks: Ability to craft custom materials (post-MVP feature)

**When:** Tier 4 (Lead Guide)

---

### 6. Perfect Morning Routine (All Children Settled in 15 Minutes)
**What it is:** Drop-off time (7:45-8:00am). Get all children from arrival → coat off → say goodbye to parent → choose first work

**Challenge:** Some kids have separation anxiety, some parents linger, some kids bolt for the door

**Reward:**
- +1 Discovery Token
- Trust +5
- Sets positive tone for whole day (children start with +mood boost)
- Achievement: "Smooth Transitions"

**When:** Tier 2+

---

### 7. Child Graduates to Primary Class
**What it is:** Elijah (or another child) turns 3 and moves to the 3-6 classroom. Bittersweet moment.

**Challenge:** Preparing them emotionally, celebrating their growth

**Reward:**
- +5 Discovery Tokens (huge milestone)
- Trust +20
- Emotional cutscene (letters from child/parents)
- Achievement: "Watched Them Grow"
- New toddler arrives to fill the spot

**When:** Tier 3-4, happens naturally after ~20 in-game days

---

### 8. Full Day Without Intervention from Lead Guide
**What it is:** Lead guide observes you all day but never has to step in

**Challenge:** Handle every situation (meltdowns, conflicts, accidents, boredom) competently

**Reward:**
- +5 Discovery Tokens
- Trust +25 (maxes out at 100%)
- Lead guide says "You're ready" (unlocks Tier 4)
- Achievement: "Independent Guide"

**When:** Tier 3 → Tier 4 transition

### 9. Outdoor Education Success
**What it is:** Take children outside for nature walk, everyone stays safe and engaged

**Challenge:** Toddlers run in different directions, some eat dirt, some get scared of bugs

**Reward:**
- +2 Discovery Tokens
- Trust +5
- Children gain +nature knowledge, +gross motor skills
- Achievement: "Nature Guide"

**When:** Tier 2+

### 10. Successfully Complete a Full Week
**What it is:** Monday through Friday without major incidents

**Challenge:** Consistency, energy management (you get tired too!)

**Reward:**
- +3 Discovery Tokens
- +10 Stars
- Trust +10
- Weekend reflection scene (journal, rest, prepare for next week)
- Achievement: "Week One Complete" (first time), "Seasoned Guide" (10 weeks)

**When:** Any tier, recurring milestone

## Montessori Philosophy Integration

**Key Teaching Moments (Educational for Players):**

1. **"Follow the Child"** — When you try to force a lesson a child isn't ready for, it fails. Game teaches you to observe first.

2. **"Prepared Environment"** — If the room is messy or materials are broken, children can't work well. Game teaches environmental care.

3. **"Independence Over Dependence"** — Doing things FOR children (tying shoes, opening snacks) feels helpful but actually hinders. Game rewards letting them struggle productively.

4. **"Freedom Within Limits"** — Children can choose any work, but must follow ground rules (one material at a time, return when done). Game teaches balance.

5. **"Sensitive Periods"** — Children learn explosively when developmentally ready, but you can't force it early. Game teaches timing.

## Progression Summary: Assistant to Lead Guide

**Tier 1: Observer (Days 1-2)**
- Milestone: Complete observation journals for all 12 children
- Learn: Who the children are, what they need

**Tier 2: Assistant (Days 3-5)**
- Milestones: First three-period lesson, first perfect morning routine, help with nap time
- Learn: Basic Montessori techniques

**Tier 3: Guide-in-Training (Days 6-10)**
- Milestones: Child's first independent work cycle, successful conflict redirect, parent education night, manage half of nap time solo
- Learn: Deeper philosophy, environmental design, parent communication

**Tier 4: Lead Guide (Days 11+)**
- Milestones: Full day solo, perfect nap time, child graduation, custom material creation, full week completion
- Learn: Mastery, mentorship, curriculum design
