# Material Acquisition & Collection System

**Date:** 2026-02-07
**Context:** Gacha-inspired mechanics for acquiring Montessori materials (no real money)

---

## Core Philosophy

**NOT predatory gacha** — This is about discovery, surprise, and collection joy, NOT gambling addiction. Inspired by:
- **Animal Crossing:** Catalog orders, random visitors, seasonal rotation
- **Stardew Valley:** Pierre's shop + traveling cart combo
- **Genshin Impact:** Multiple currencies, pity system, progression-based unlocks
- **Pokémon:** Rarity tiers, collection completion incentives

---

## Currency System

### 1. Stars ⭐ (Primary Currency)
**How you earn:**
- Successful lesson presentations (+1-3 stars based on quality)
- Children having "aha moments" (+5 stars)
- Daily login bonus (+10 stars)
- Completing observation journal entries (+2 stars each)

**What it buys:**
- Standard catalog materials (guaranteed, but limited selection)
- Crafting supplies
- Room upgrades

**Feel:** Steady, predictable income for effort

---

### 2. Discovery Tokens 🎟️ (Gacha Currency)
**How you earn:**
- Level up rewards (1-3 tokens)
- Fixing a non-Montessori practice (+2 tokens)
- Perfect week (all children made progress) (+5 tokens)
- Seasonal events (+3-10 tokens)
- Achievements (+1-5 tokens)

**What it buys:**
- Pull from the "Conference Trunk" (random material gacha)
- Pull from limited-time event pools

**Feel:** Special, exciting, slightly rare

---

### 3. Trust Meter 💙 (Relationship Currency)
**How it grows:**
- Successful lessons when lead guide is watching
- Handling difficult situations well
- Showing improvement over time
- Having positive parent conversations

**What it unlocks:**
- At certain thresholds, lead guide gives you vintage/heirloom materials from her personal collection (guaranteed quality items)
- Higher trust = better selection in school budget orders
- Unlocks mentorship conversations (tutorials/tips)

**Feel:** Emotional progression, relationship-based rewards

---

## Acquisition Methods

### Method 1: School Budget Order (Monthly)
**Timing:** Once per in-game month (~every 5-7 real-world play sessions)

**How it works:**
- School gets a budget for new materials
- You get to select 3 items from a curated list of 12
- Your Trust Meter determines the quality of the list (higher trust = better options, including rare items)
- Costs stars, but reasonable amounts

**Inspiration:** Stardew Valley's Pierre's shop + catalog system

**Player choice:** Guaranteed acquisition, but must choose wisely (can't get everything)

---

### Method 2: Conference Trunk (Gacha Pull)
**Timing:** Whenever you have Discovery Tokens

**How it works:**
- Spend 1 token for a single pull, 10 tokens for a 10-pull (guaranteed 1 rare+)
- Randomized material from the full pool
- Rarity distribution:
  - 🪵 Handmade: 50% chance
  - 🌿 Classic: 35% chance
  - ✨ Heirloom: 13% chance
  - 🌟 Legacy: 2% chance

**Pity system (Genshin-inspired):**
- Every 10 pulls, guaranteed 🌿 Classic or better
- Every 30 pulls, guaranteed ✨ Heirloom or better
- Every 90 pulls, guaranteed 🌟 Legacy
- Pity counter persists across sessions

**Inspiration:** Genshin Impact wishes, Fire Emblem Heroes summoning

**Player excitement:** The thrill of the pull, but with safety nets

---

### Method 3: Traveling Artisan (Random Event)
**Timing:** Random, ~once per week (in-game)

**How it works:**
- A woodworker/craftsperson visits the school
- Offers 3-5 unique materials (often themed - e.g., "all wooden sensory materials")
- Accepts stars OR you can trade materials you don't need
- Sometimes offers custom commissions (pay now, receive in 3 days)

**Inspiration:** Stardew Valley's traveling cart, Animal Crossing's Redd/Saharah

**Player agency:** Trading system adds depth, limited-time creates urgency without pressure

---

### Method 4: DIY Crafting
**Timing:** Anytime you have supplies

**How it works:**
- Collect crafting materials from:
  - Outdoor time with children (pinecones, leaves, stones)
  - Town errands (when that unlocks)
  - Traveling artisan sales
- Craft basic materials yourself (takes in-game time)
- Crafted items are always 🪵 Handmade quality, but you choose exactly what you need
- Recipes unlock through:
  - Montessori books (research)
  - Workshops (professional development)
  - Trial and error

**Inspiration:** Animal Crossing crafting, Stardew Valley machines

**Player satisfaction:** Self-sufficiency, control, learning

---

### Method 5: Trust Gifts (Relationship Rewards)
**Timing:** When you hit trust milestones

**How it works:**
- At 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% trust, lead guide gives you a special item from her collection
- Always ✨ Heirloom or 🌟 Legacy quality
- Often has a story attached (cutscene or dialogue)
- Example: "This Pink Tower was hand-sanded by Maria Montessori's apprentice in 1920..."

**Inspiration:** Stardew Valley's heart events, Persona social links

**Player emotion:** Feels earned, meaningful, story-driven

---

### Method 6: Achievement Chests
**Timing:** When you complete milestones

**How it works:**
- Complete collections ("All Practical Life materials")
- Complete challenges ("Present 50 successful lessons")
- Discover all children's sensitive periods
- Rewards: Discovery Tokens, rare materials, cosmetic unlocks

**Inspiration:** Every modern game with achievements

**Player engagement:** Long-term goals, completionist satisfaction

---

## Rarity Tiers & Effects (Reminder)

### 🪵 Handmade (Common)
- Basic functionality
- Simple pixel art
- Children use them, but not drawn to them
- Short concentration time

### 🌿 Classic (Uncommon)
- Standard Montessori quality
- Polished pixel art
- Children choose them regularly
- Normal concentration time

### ✨ Heirloom (Rare)
- Beautiful, detailed sprites
- Special animation when in use (subtle glow, sparkles)
- Children drawn to them more often
- Longer concentration time
- May trigger special "aha moment" cutscenes

### 🌟 Legacy (Very Rare)
- Stunning art, unique visual flair
- Full animated scenes when used
- Children LOVE these materials
- Significantly longer concentration
- Guaranteed "aha moment" potential
- Often comes with lore/story

## Balancing for MVP

**For the first release, focus on:**
1. **School Budget Order** (Method 1) - Core acquisition loop
2. **Conference Trunk** (Method 2) - Gacha excitement
3. **Trust Gifts** (Method 5) - Emotional progression

**Save for post-MVP:**
- Traveling Artisan (adds complexity)
- DIY Crafting (requires resource gathering systems)
- Achievement Chests (need more content to make worthwhile)

**Starting materials:**
- You begin with 2-3 🪵 Handmade Practical Life materials
- Lead guide has a classroom with mostly 🌿 Classic materials
- You're building your own collection to eventually run your own room
