🎨 Floor Texture - Review Before Cleanup

Single Tile (128x128 at 2x scale)

This is ONE tile at 2x zoom. Look for:
  • 2-3 LARGE vertical planks (not many small ones)
  • Warm orange/brown color (#D8894C)
  • Simple wood grain (horizontal lines)
  • Clean pixel art style (not blurry/realistic)

Tiled Pattern (3x3 grid to test seamless tiling)

This shows the tile repeated 3x3. Check:
  • Seamless edges - Do planks align where tiles meet?
  • No visible seams - Should look like one continuous floor
  • Pattern repeats nicely - Not jarring or obvious

✅ Review Checklist

Before approving this floor, verify:

  • ☐ Planks are LARGE (40-60px wide each, not tiny)
  • ☐ 2-3 planks visible in the tile (not 6-8 small ones)
  • ☐ Planks run vertically (top to bottom)
  • ☐ Tiles seamlessly when repeated (no visible seams)
  • ☐ Warm orange/brown wood color
  • ☐ Stardew Valley pixel art style
  • ☐ Simple horizontal grain lines (not too detailed)
